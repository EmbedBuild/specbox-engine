# Services and support

SpecBox Engine is **fair source** under the [FSL-1.1-ALv2](LICENSE). You can use the engine, the
extension, the CLI and the FreeForm board for free, **also in production and in your clients'
projects**, without signing anything. The only use it does not allow is offering a commercial product
or service that competes with SpecBox. Each version becomes Apache 2.0 two years after its release.

If you want help, we offer:

- **Consulting**: how to bring spec-driven development with Claude Code to your team or your clients.
- **Implementation**: setting up SpecBox in your projects, with your stack, your board and your quality gates.
- **Training**: for teams that start with SpecBox and Claude Code.
- **Support**: priority answers and fixes for your installation.

SpecBox Cloud (the shared board and the panel), SpecBox Projects (the portal for your clients) and SpecBox
Mobile are paid products, with prices coming soon.

Write to **hola@specbox.build** or visit [embed.build](https://www.embed.build).
